Project

2023 – 2024

AsyQuote, construction SaaS platform

Final-year project, Colégio Internato dos Carvalhos. Grade 20/20. Report (46 pages, Portuguese).

Quoting software for small construction firms, which usually build proposals by hand in spreadsheets. A quote is a tree of chapters, services and priced lines, with cost and charged price tracked per line. The Excel export uses live formulas, so the client can change a quantity and watch the total follow.

Data
An asyncio/aiohttp scraper builds the supplier price catalogue, fetching pages in batches of ten and retrying 429 and 5xx responses with exponential backoff. A full run collected about 40,000 products, with their pages parsed and images downloaded, in around 15 minutes. In 2026 I made it check every URL against robots.txt with a matcher written to RFC 9309 (Python's robotparser mishandles wildcards) and honour the site's Crawl-delay.
Auth
Email verification before first use, reCAPTCHA on signup, Argon2 instead of Django's default hasher, Django's password validators including its 20,000 most common passwords, and reset links that expire after two minutes.
